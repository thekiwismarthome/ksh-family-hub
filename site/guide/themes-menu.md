# Change the look and the menu

Menu (☰, top right) → **Settings**.

![Themes](/shots/themes.webp)

## Light or dark

**Appearance** has three choices: **Dark**, **Light** and **Match Home Assistant** (the hub follows Home Assistant's own setting). This is per device.

## Ten themes

**Theme** has ten colour themes, each with a light and a dark version. The colour appears on the tabs, buttons and the page you are on; the backgrounds stay calm.

| Theme | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| Graphite | Neutral grey and soft black |
| Fintech blue and green | Trustworthy blue with a green accent (the default) |
| Indigo calendar | Serene indigo with mint |
| Fresh food green | Garden and kitchen green |
| Health and wellness | Calm teal |
| Delivery orange | Warm, energetic orange |
| Coral dating | Friendly coral |
| Social pink and purple | Magenta and pink |
| Roasted café | Coffee browns and cream |
| Lavender meditation | Soft lavender |

The themes come from the [mobile app colour palettes on paletadecolores.org](https://paletadecolores.org/en/mobile-app-color-palette.html); the dark versions are made from them with the tint toned down.

## Order the menu

**Menu** in Settings lists every part. Use the arrows to **move** one up or down and the switch to **show or hide** it. The first three after Home appear in the phone's bottom bar, the rest in **More**.

On a tablet the same order is the icon rail down the left. The order is saved on the server, so it is the same on every device.

## Switch whole parts off

The switches in **Menu** (administrators only) switch parts of the hub on and off (Shopping, Meals, Chores, Calendar, Finance). Switching a part off keeps its data. The integration's **Configure** button has the same switches.

## Hide things from certain people

**Who can see what** (administrators only): pick a person, then switch off the menu entries they should not see. It keeps the menu tidy for each person wherever they use the hub, including a shared tablet while they are picked.

::: warning Tidy, not a lock
This hides menu entries. It is not a security lock: someone who knows Home Assistant can still reach the data. Do not rely on it to hide anything that must stay private.
:::

## Region and language

Menu → **Change Region/Language**: the interface language of the shopping and meals parts and the country of the product catalogue.
