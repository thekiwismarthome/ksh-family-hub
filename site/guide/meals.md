# Recipes and the meal planner

![Meals](/shots/meals.webp)

## Your recipes

The **Meals** part is your recipe book. The button at the top switches between **tiles** and a **list**; the choice is remembered (and is in Meals settings → Recipe view).

- Search at the top. Filter by **Courses**, **Categories**, **Collections** or tag with the chips, and by star rating with the filter button.
- **Favourites** sit in their own section at the top. Tap the heart on any recipe.
- The **Tonight** card shows what is planned for dinner, with a **Cook** button (or **Plan** when nothing is planned) and how many dinners are planned this week.

## Add a recipe

Press **+** and choose how:

- **From a web page**: paste the address of any recipe website. The hub reads the ingredients, steps, times and photo. Check it, then save.
- **From Recipe Keeper**: export from the Recipe Keeper app and import the file here.
- **By hand**: name, photo, ingredients, steps, times, servings, tags.

## Cooking

Open a recipe to **scale** the servings (the amounts change with them), switch between metric and imperial, and start **timers**. Timers keep running when you leave the recipe (see **Timers** in the Meals tabs). **Keep screen awake** (the eye button) stops the phone sleeping while you cook.

## The meal planner

**Planner** shows the week. Tap a day and a meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack) and pick a recipe. Tonight's dinner shows on the Home page.

## To the shopping list

Open a recipe and choose **Add to shopping list**: its ingredients are shown with nothing ticked, so you tick only what you need (you probably have the salt), and they go on the list you pick.

## Settings

Meals settings (the gear) has the **units** (metric or imperial), the number of columns for tiles, whether to show favourites and the planner, timer sounds and language.
