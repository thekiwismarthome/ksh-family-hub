# Points, rewards and pocket money

Chores earn **points**. Points can be **spent on rewards**, and they can also turn into **pocket money** that is paid into the child's accounts.

![Points](/shots/points.webp)

## Points

**Chores → Points** shows everyone's balance and a history. An adult can add or take away points with the **±** button (for a bonus, or to correct a mistake).

## Rewards to spend points on

1. **Chores → Points → Spend your points**, as an adult: **Add a reward**.
2. Start from the 12 standard ones (extra screen time, pick the movie, ice cream, a new toy…) or create your own with a name, an emoji and a cost.
3. A child taps **Ask for it**. The points are held while they wait.
4. An adult **approves** (the points are spent) or **declines** (they come back).

## Pocket money rules

For each child, an adult sets: how many **points** they need to earn **per week or per month**, and how much **pocket money** that earns, and into which account (Cash or Bank).

**Chores → Points → Pocket money → the child.** Weeks start on Monday. The first week that counts is the first full week after you turn it on.

- Only points from chores, routines and corrections count. Bonuses and spending do not.
- When the week (or month) finishes and the child reached the target, the amount is paid into their account automatically, once. If Finance is switched off, nothing is paid and it is paid later.
- The child sees progress as a bar: "14 of 20 stars this week. 6 more for $5.00".

## Finance: Cash and Bank

**Finance** shows a card for each child with their **Cash**, **Bank** and **total**, the pocket money progress and recent activity.

![Finance](/shots/finance.webp)

Adults can:

- **Money in** (a gift, or pocket money by hand) into Cash or Bank.
- **Spent**: record what was bought and the price. Pay it from **Cash**, from **Bank**, or **Cash first, then Bank**. A purchase that uses both is shown as one entry. It will not go below zero.
- **Move**: take money out of the bank into cash, or the other way.
- **Undo**: open any entry and void it.

A child sees only their own money. A guest sees no money at all. The currency is the one set in Home Assistant.

**History** lists every entry, grouped by day, with the emoji and what it was spent on.
