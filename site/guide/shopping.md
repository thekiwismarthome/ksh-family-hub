# Shopping lists

![Shopping](/shots/shopping.webp)

The **Shopping** part is the Shopping List Manager you may know, inside the hub.

## The list

- The card at the top shows **Left to buy** and the **Estimated** total of the list, with a progress bar.
- Type in the **search bar** to find a product from the catalogue (about 170 products for most countries, with fuzzy search: "yogurt" finds "Yogurt - Greek") or add your own.
- Tap an item to tick it off. Items are grouped by **category** (Fruit & Veg, Dairy & Eggs…). Changes appear on every device straight away.
- The button next to the three dots switches between **tiles** and a **list**. The three dots hold **Share list**, **Sort** (by category or A to Z), **Show price**, **Recently used**, **Keep screen on** and **Clear checked items**.
- Use the camera button in the search bar to **scan a barcode** and look the product up.

## Lists

The **Lists** tab shows all your lists. Create as many as you like (the weekly shop, the hardware store, a party).
Lists can be shared with the **household** or kept private.

## Region and catalogue

Menu (☰) → **Change Region/Language** picks the country whose product catalogue is used. Products you created yourself are kept when you switch. **Custom regions…** on the same sheet makes a region of your own.

## Supermarkets and loyalty cards

- **Settings → Supermarkets** (gear in the Shopping tabs) lists the chains in your region with their address, so a list can say where you shop.
- **Cards** (in the menu) is your **loyalty card wallet**: add a card by scanning or typing its barcode, give it a picture, and show the barcode full screen at the till.

## Households

If more than one family uses the same Home Assistant, **households** keep lists and cards private to each (menu → the household name at the top). An administrator creates a household and adds its members.

## Add to the list from a recipe

Open a recipe in **Meals** and use **Add to shopping list**, then pick the ingredients you need. See [Recipes and the meal planner](./meals).
